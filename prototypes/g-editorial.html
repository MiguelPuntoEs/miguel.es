Builds with language models by day; teaches economics, Python and blockchain the rest of the time.

Senior LLM Engineer, A5 Labs · Madrid → California, remotely

The path wasn't straight: aerospace engineering first, then signal theory, four countries of software and data work — weather at AEMet, satellites at Airbus, precision instruments at Leica — before settling into data science and, for the last years, LLM engineering.

The economics came later, deliberately: an MA at Universidad de las Hespérides, a thesis on the quantity theory of money, and now courses at three universities plus a set of calculators on live OECD data that students and strangers actually use.

"Aero → signals → data → LLMs. The tools change; the engineering doesn't." — the short version of the CV
